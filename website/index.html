Why Choose LiveTranslate

Ultra-Low Latency

Powered by streaming APIs from Google Gemini 2.0 and Azure Cognitive Services for near-instantantaneous translations.

A sleek, semi-transparent floating window that stays on top of your meetings, games, or videos.

Secure & Private

Use your own personal API keys. Data is directly streamed to the AI provider, and keys are stored locally.
